OntoGraph

GraphRAG over an enterprise knowledge graph: an OWL/RDFS ontology of the Order-to-Cash process, quality-gated with SHACL shapes and queried with SPARQL. Natural-language questions become generated SPARQL, traverse the graph, and ground the LLM on retrieved facts — so "how much do APAC customers still owe?" is a SUM over a traversal, and every number traces back to a triple.
