How I work an incident

The same five steps, every time

  1. 01Listen

    Work out what the customer is actually trying to do — not just what broke.

  2. 02Reproduce

    Get it to fail on my side, with logs, payloads and timestamps.

  3. 03Isolate

    API, data, config, network or device — narrow it to one layer.

  4. 04Resolve or escalate

    Fix it, or hand R&D a clean repro with impact attached.

  5. 05Close the loop

    Update the customer, then the knowledge base, so it doesn't recur.
